Delete Your Account

Last updated: May 26, 2026

You can delete your MyTask AI account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how.

Option 1: Delete in the App (Recommended)

The fastest way is directly inside the MyTask AI app:

  1. Open the MyTask AI app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the Account section.
  5. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all data are deleted immediately and irreversibly.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to:

nicexex@gmail.com

Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and send the email from the same address that is linked to your MyTask AI account (Google or Apple). We will verify your identity and delete the account within 30 days, typically much sooner.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your profile (name, email, language, avatar color)
  • All tasks and subtasks you created
  • Your entire AI chat history
  • All images you uploaded (chat photos, task photos)
  • Saved templates
  • Push-notification tokens
  • Subscription records linked to your account
  • Your authentication record (Google / Apple Sign-In link)

Data Retention

Account deletion is immediate. The following minimal data may be retained briefly for legitimate reasons:

  • Server logs: automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups: overwritten on the regular backup rotation (maximum 30 days).
  • Legal/financial records: if you made a purchase, anonymised transaction records may be retained as required by tax and accounting law (typically up to 10 years), but these no longer contain personally identifiable account data.

Subscriptions

Deleting your MyTask AI account does not automatically cancel an active subscription billed by Google Play or Apple. To cancel the subscription itself, manage it through your Google Play or Apple subscription settings before deleting the account.

Contact

For any questions about account deletion or your data rights, contact nicexex@gmail.com.
